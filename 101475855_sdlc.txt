The Importance of DevOps in the SDLC

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) includes different stages such as planning, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance.

DevOps improves this process by encouraging communication between development and operations teams.

Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) help automate testing and deployment activities.

In my opinion, DevOps is important because it makes software development more organized, efficient, and reliable.

Another benefit of DevOps is that it supports continuous feedback throughout the software development lifecycle.

Instead of waiting until the end of a project to identify problems, teams can use automated testing and continuous integration to detect issues earlier.

I believe this approach helps developers improve software quality while making the development process more efficient.
